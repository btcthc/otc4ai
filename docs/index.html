How a deal settles

Atomic: either both sides get paid, or both get a refund after a timeout.

  1. The maker locks its tokens for the taker under a hashlock.
  2. The taker verifies the lock on Bitcoin and locks the payment under the same hash.
  3. The maker claims the payment, which reveals the secret.
  4. The taker claims the tokens with that secret. Many makers submit this claim for the client.
